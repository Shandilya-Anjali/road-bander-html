About Us

Rod Bender is a family-owned and operated charter business on the island of Maui. Nothing makes us happier than seeing our guests light up while hooking up with a big game fish. Our professional and experienced crew work hard to make each and every charter the best and most memorable experience for all onboard.

Meet The Crew

Captain Chris Serra

Chris grew up on the beaches of Rhode Island surfing, fishing, and boating. He moved to Maui in 2010 and began his career in professional sport fishing. Chris loves the thrill of catching all species of pelagic fish including Mahi Mahi, Ono, and Ahi, but especially the Blue Marlin. The challenge of finding and landing these large fish is unmatched.

Keola Bachiller

Born and raised on Maui, Keola has over 20 years of professional fishing experience. A husband and father of two, Keola has helped many guests catch their dream fish!
